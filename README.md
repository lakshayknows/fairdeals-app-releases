# FairDeals for Android

Order packaged drinking water and Lahori drinks by the crate in South Delhi.

## Download

**[Download the latest FairDeals app (APK)](https://github.com/lakshayknows/fairdeals-app-releases/releases/latest/download/FairDeals.apk)**

Needs Android 7.0 or newer. About 3 MB.

## Install

1. Open the download on your phone and tap **FairDeals.apk**.
2. If Android asks, allow your browser to **install unknown apps**, then go back and tap **Install**.
3. Open **FairDeals** and sign in, or create an account.

The app always shows the live FairDeals shop, so you don't need to reinstall it to get new products, prices or features.

## Prefer the website?

Everything in the app also works at [fairdeals365.com](https://fairdeals365.com).
